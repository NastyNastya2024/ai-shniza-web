C O P Y · P A S T E · G E N E R A T E

Rebuild this photo as a Rockstar Games character render from
Grand Theft Auto VI. Not a filtered photo — a 3D game asset,
rendered.

Keep who she is and where she is: same facial identity, same
pose, same outfit, same camera angle, same composition, same
background layout. But rebuild every element as game geometry
and game shaders.

Her face and body become a high-poly hero character model:
sculpted, slightly idealised features, clean defined jaw and
cheekbone forms, smoothed skin with a subsurface-scattering
shader rather than photographic pores, soft specular sheen on
the forehead, nose and lips, subtle sculpted asymmetry. Skin
reads as beautifully shaded 3D, never as a photograph, never
as a cartoon.

Hair becomes styled game hair — solid sculpted volume with
defined clumps and card-based strands, glossy specular
highlight running across the top, clean silhouette against
the background.

Clothing becomes game-engine fabric: simplified clean folds,
slightly stiff drape, crisp seams and stitching, believable
material response without photographic fibre detail.

The whole world takes on Rockstar's visual signature — Vice
City, present day. Hot saturated Miami palette: turquoise,
coral, sun-bleached pastels, hard blue sky, warm concrete.
Strong low tropical sun throwing long directional shadows,
powerful orange rim light separating her from the background,
deep ambient occlusion in every crease, warm haze and heat
shimmer in the distance. Colour is pushed and vivid — richer
than reality, controlled and stylish rather than neon.

Background simplifies into readable game environment: clean
architectural geometry, palm trees as game assets, prop
clutter and signage with thick tangible edges, painted road
markings, slight distance fog, background elements losing
micro-detail the further they sit from camera.

Render quality: ray-traced global illumination, screen-space
reflections, crisp anti-aliased edges, high contrast, deep
saturated blacks, glossy highlight rolloff, a faint filmic
grain over everything, subtle vignette.

The final image should be instantly recognisable as a video
game — a frame from a AAA open-world title on max settings.
Not a photo, not a photo with a grade, not concept art, not
anime, not cel shading, not cyberpunk neon. No HUD, no text,
no watermark, no changed pose, no changed outfit, no
reframing.
